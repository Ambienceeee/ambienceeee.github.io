Privacy policy

Effective September 25, 2026.

Ambience Client ("the launcher") is a desktop app that runs on your computer. This policy explains what it does with your information. In short, everything stays on your computer. We don't run a server and we don't collect data.

What the launcher stores

The launcher keeps the following on your computer, in its data folder or the system keyring:

  • Sign-in tokens from Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services, so you stay signed in and can launch the game. They are kept in the system keyring where one is available, or otherwise in a file readable only by your user account.
  • Your Minecraft profile: username and UUID, and whether the account owns Minecraft: Java Edition.
  • Your setup: instances, game files, mods, settings and HUD layout.

The launcher never asks for your Microsoft password. You enter it only on Microsoft's own sign-in page.

What we collect

Nothing. The launcher has no analytics, telemetry, tracking or advertising. There is no Ambience account and no Ambience server, so none of the data above is ever sent to us.

Who the launcher talks to

To do its job, the launcher connects directly from your computer to these services, under their own privacy policies:

  • Microsoft and Xbox Live (login.microsoftonline.com, user.auth.xboxlive.com, xsts.auth.xboxlive.com): signing in.
  • Minecraft services and Mojang (api.minecraftservices.com, piston-meta.mojang.com and Mojang's download servers): checking ownership, reading your profile and downloading the game.
  • Minecraft skins (textures.minecraft.net): loading your own skin image to show your head in the account menu.
  • Fabric and Quilt (meta.fabricmc.net, maven.fabricmc.net, meta.quiltmc.org): loader versions, if you use them.
  • Modrinth (api.modrinth.com): only when you search for or install a mod.
  • mcsrvstat.us (api.mcsrvstat.us): the public status and player count of the featured server on the home screen.

These requests contain only what each service needs, such as sign-in tokens for Microsoft or a search term for Modrinth.

Removing your data

Removing an account from the launcher deletes its stored tokens. Uninstalling the launcher and deleting its data folder removes everything it stored. You can also revoke the launcher's access to your Microsoft account at any time at account.live.com/consent/Manage.

Children

The launcher doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
